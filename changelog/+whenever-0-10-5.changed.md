Raised the `whenever` requirement from `>=0.9.3,<0.10.0` to `>=0.10.5,<0.12.0`, so the SDK can be installed with Prefect 3.9, which requires `whenever` 0.10.5 or later. With `whenever` 0.10.5 and later, `Timestamp` accepts a time with second 60, such as `2016-12-31T23:59:60Z`, and reads it as second 59, where it used to raise `TimestampFormatError`. With `whenever` 0.11, `Timestamp` matches time zone names regardless of letter case: `2022-01-01T11:00:00+01:00[europe/paris]` keeps the `Europe/Paris` time zone where it used to be converted to UTC, and the same string without the offset is accepted where it used to raise `TimestampFormatError`. Also with `whenever` 0.11, when `add()` or `subtract()` shifts a `Timestamp` in a time zone other than UTC by years, months, weeks or days into a time that occurs twice because of a daylight saving change, the shifted time keeps the offset of the starting time, where earlier versions picked the earlier of the two times; a shift that also includes hours or smaller units can therefore end one hour later.
